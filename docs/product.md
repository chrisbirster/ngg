# Product

NGG is a place for small, distinctive browser games and the creators behind them. It borrows the dense discovery and community energy of classic web portals without copying their artwork, branding, or assets.

## First vertical slice

`/games/xo-arena-football` includes:

- global navigation and game categories;
- playable local X/O football presentation;
- creator and runtime metadata;
- related games;
- reactions, comments, playlists, sharing, and reporting;
- responsive desktop/mobile layouts;
- an original promotional banner.

The first question is whether a visitor can discover a game, understand it, start playing, and engage with its creator without friction.
